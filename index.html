Community Plant Swap Registration

For plant lovers of any experience level who want to trade houseplants, cuttings, and herbs. Registering saves your spot and helps us plan table space. It takes about 3 minutes.

F1 · Purpose and Expectations

  • After you submit: you'll get a confirmation email with your session time and swap tips.
  • Required fields are marked "(required)". Everything else is optional.
  • Privacy: We only ask for your first name and email, and we use them only for this swap. This is a class project, so please use made-up information.

State matrix

How each form state is shown and what the user should do
State What causes it How it is shown Entry kept? What to do next
Default Field has not been used yet. Gray border with a visible label. N/A Fill in the field.
Focus User clicks or tabs into the field. Thick dark outline around the field. Yes Type or choose an answer.
Required The field must be filled before submitting. "(required)" written in the label. Yes Complete the field before submitting.
Valid Entry meets the field's rules. "✓ Looks good." text under the field. Yes Move to the next field.
Invalid Entry breaks a rule, such as 8 plants when the limit is 5. "Error:" message, red border, and red bar on the left. Yes, the entry stays in the field. Follow the message and change the entry.
Disabled Option is not available, such as a full session. Grayed out and cannot be selected; label says "(full)". N/A Choose a different option.
Server-returned error The fictional server rejected the email "sam.example" because it is missing "@" and a domain. Error summary at the top with a link to the field, plus an "Error:" message, red border, and red bar. Yes, "sam.example" stays in the field so the user can fix it. Click the summary link, correct the email to a full address such as name@example.com, and submit again.
